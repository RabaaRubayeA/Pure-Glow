FAQ

Are your products suitable for all skin types?

Yes. Our skincare products are carefully formulated to be gentle and effective for all skin types, including dry, oily, combination, and sensitive skin. We always recommend performing a patch test before first use.

How long does it take to see visible results?

While every skin is unique, most customers notice improved hydration and smoother skin within the first week. For brighter, healthier-looking skin, consistent use for 4–6 weeks is recommended.

Are your products made with natural ingredients?

Absolutely. We use high-quality botanical extracts and skin-loving ingredients that are carefully selected to nourish and protect your skin. Our formulas are free from harsh chemicals and are designed for daily use.
